What they are

TopoRescue Maps provides search and rescue (SAR) and civil protection teams with free, very detailed topographic maps built from the Italian regional technical maps at 1:5,000 scale, digital terrain models and OpenStreetMap: buildings, trails, roads, contour lines, streams, power lines, cable cars and ski lifts, place and peak names.

They are made for missing person searches and for ground teams, search dogs (K9 units) and mountain rescue: know the terrain before entering the area, cover the assigned sector with the GPS, hand back the tracks as proof of coverage.

Formats

  • Garmin maps (.img) for handheld GPS units and dog tracking collars (for example Atemos, GPSMAP, Alpha), by province and by municipality, loaded with the Carica Mappe program or from the browser; also viewable on the computer with QMapShack.
  • DXF for AutoCAD in metres (UTM), in colour and black and white, and DXF in degrees (WGS84) for Global Mapper and GPS.
  • GeoPackage and ready-made QGIS projects with orthophotos, UTM grid, 1:5,000 print layouts and loading of the GPX files of the assigned sectors.
  • 3D terrain in QGIS, to assess slopes, cliffs and gullies – the difficulty and safety of an area before entering it.
  • Works offline: the Mac and Windows apps keep a copy of the area's maps on the computer.

Coverage

Italy: all regions – Friuli Venezia Giulia, Veneto, Trentino, South Tyrol, Aosta Valley, Piedmont, Lombardy, Liguria, Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany, Marche, Umbria, Lazio, Abruzzo, Molise, Campania, Apulia, Basilicata, Calabria, Sicily, Sardinia.

Neighbouring countries: Switzerland, Austria, Slovenia and the French border departments.

How to get them

  1. Free registration, stating your organisation (civil protection group, mountain rescue, fire brigade, Red Cross, police, public bodies).
  2. At the first sign-in you read and accept the disclaimer.
  3. Use the web pages (nothing to install) or the Mac and Windows apps: Carica Mappe for Garmin GPS, Estrarre Mappe for DXF, QGIS and 3D.
  4. The full local copy for offline work is reserved for recognised rescue organisations.

Sources and limits

The data come from public sources: technical maps of the Italian regions and of the neighbouring countries, digital terrain models, OpenStreetMap, under their own licences. The terrain may have changed: the maps do not replace checking on site or the instructions of whoever coordinates the operation.
